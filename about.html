About Us

TLC for Kids is a licensed daycare center located in Springville, UT.

At TLC for Kids, we're all about helping little ones learn and grow! We keep things fun and educational with awesome teachers in a super safe space. We have been a trusted partner for families in our community for many years and we understand the unique challenges faced by parents today, especially those juggling work and family life. That's why we offer comprehensive childcare for children from infancy to pre-teen, providing a safe and nurturing haven where they can blossom. We know it's tough leaving your kiddo, so our goal is to make it a blast for them (and give you some peace of mind too!). At TLC for Kids, we're more than just childcare – we're laying the foundation for a bright future, one giggle and exploration at a time. We're like a big, happy family here, and we'd love for yours to join us!

Our Mission

TLC for Kids is dedicated to fostering a nurturing and secure environment where children can thrive. Through high-quality care and engaging experiences, we empower each child's unique developmental journey, preparing them for success in future learning endeavors.
