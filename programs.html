Programs

0–24 Months Old

Welcome little snuggle bugs! Our Infant program at TLC for Kids is like a big hug for your precious babe. Our rockstar teachers (seriously, they're amazing with little ones!) are all about keeping things cozy and cuddly. We'll play peek-a-boo, sing silly songs, and give your tiny adventurer all the tummy time they can handle. It's all about helping your baby grow and learn through play and lots of snuggles. We know leaving your little one can be tough, so we aim to make it a smooth transition for everyone – you and your little explorer! It's the perfect place for your baby's big adventure to begin!

2 Years Old

Two-year-olds? We've got 'em covered at TLC for Kids' Twos program! It's a fun-filled place built just for your busy adventurer. Our teachers are rockstars at creating a safe space where your little one can explore, sing, play, and make new friends. We'll be learning all sorts of cool stuff – colors, shapes, numbers, sharing – but mostly, it's about having a blast! Let the fun begin!

3 Years Old

Buckle up for tons of fun in our Threes program at TLC for Kids! It's basically playtime central for your little superstar. We've got awesome teachers who make learning feel like a giant adventure. Think singing, stories, messy art projects (don't worry, we clean up!), and tons of playtime with new friends. Your little one will be counting like a pro, rocking their ABCs, and learning all sorts of cool stuff – all while having a blast, of course! We'll also help them navigate sharing toys, making friends, and becoming a confident little chatterbox. At TLC for Kids, threes is all about laughter, learning, and getting ready for preschool – one giggle at a time!

4–12 Years Old

After school's out, the fun is just beginning! We're all about keeping things cool for our big kids (ages 4-12) with after-school care that's epic. Think games and hanging out with new friends from all grades. It's the perfect place to unwind, recharge, and maybe even learn a magic trick or two. We'll keep your kiddos entertained until pick-up time, so you can rest easy knowing they're having a blast and getting a little boost after school.
